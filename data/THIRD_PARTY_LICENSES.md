# Third-party data

## `star_names.json`

Derived from the **IAU Catalog of Star Names (IAU-CSN)**, maintained by the
IAU Division C Working Group on Star Names (WGSN).

- Source: <https://www.pas.rochester.edu/~emamajek/WGSN/IAU-CSN.txt>
- License: IAU-produced content is released under Creative Commons
  Attribution (CC BY) — free to use as long as the source is credited. See
  <https://www.iau.org/public/themes/naming_stars/>.
- Generated by: `scripts/build_star_names.py`

## `constellation_lines.json`

Derived from Dominic Ford's (dcf21) **constellation-stick-figures** dataset
(`constellation_lines_iau.dat` and `constellation_names.dat`), which
reproduces the stick figures shown on the IAU's own constellation pages.

## Star positions/magnitudes and planetary ephemeris

Not bundled in this repo — downloaded at first run by
[Skyfield](https://rhodesmill.org/skyfield/):

- Hipparcos catalog (star positions/magnitudes), ESA 1997.
- `de421.bsp`, JPL Development Ephemeris 421 (Sun/Moon/planets), NASA JPL.
